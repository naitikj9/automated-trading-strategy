Five years overall: up 71%, down 18%, chop 11%.

## The story, briefly

- **2022:** rate hikes and a global tech selloff. Nifty flat; IT −27% while PSU banks +67%. Value and domestic cyclicals over growth.
- **2023:** the broad rally. Smallcaps and midcaps +60–78%, Realty and PSE about +80%. Almost every stock trended; trend followers had their best year.
- **2024:** the rally continued into the first half, led by Pharma, Realty and Auto; FMCG stalled.
- **2025:** choppier. Nifty +10% but with 31% of days in chop or down regimes. PSU banks and Metal led; Realty and IT fell.
- **2026 to Oct:** a large-cap bear market. Nifty −11.8% with 71% of days in the down regime. FMCG, IT, Fin Services and Bank all fell. Pharma (+19%) and Metal (+6%) held up.

## Patterns worth knowing

1. **Leadership rotates every year.** No sector stayed in the top three for all five years. Do not anchor on last year's winner.
2. **The structural winners were domestic capex and manufacturing:** PSU banks, PSE, capital goods, autos, metals. The structural losers were IT and FMCG, the old "safe" leaders.
3. **Smaller stocks trend more.** The smallcap basket was above EMA 200 on 90% of days, IT on 50%. Trend following suits small and midcaps.
4. **Returns are lumpy.** 2023 alone gave most of the five-year gain. Missing one big year changes everything.
5. **Pharma is the defensive trend.** It rose in both 2024 and the 2026 bear market, and has the lowest drawdown of the sector indices (−18.5%).
